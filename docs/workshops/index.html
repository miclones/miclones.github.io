Folding workshops

Paper and paste

Folding sessions start with a square sheet and a flat wooden tool for pressing creases. The first exercise is a plain cylinder, which teaches how a frame holds its shape and where the paper is likely to tear.

Later sessions move on to ribs bent from thin cane, joins tied with cotton thread and borders painted with a dry brush. Mistakes are pinned to a board rather than thrown away, because a crooked seam often explains more than a tidy one.

Every table has a jar of water, a cloth, two brushes and a small dish of paste. At the end of the afternoon each tool goes back into its labelled drawer.
