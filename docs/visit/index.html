Visiting

A visit to the hall takes place entirely on paper. The entrance is a green door with a brass handle, followed by a short passage where coats hang on wooden pegs and a basket collects wet umbrellas.

Beyond the passage the main room opens out. Benches run along the longest wall, the folding tables stand in the middle and the drying racks are kept near the window so that fresh glue sets in the daylight.

Allow an unhurried hour to look at the shelves. The cards beside each lantern are printed in large letters, and a magnifying glass rests on the sill for anyone curious about the grain of the paper.
