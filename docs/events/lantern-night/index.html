Lantern Night 2025

Date: 14 March 2025. This evening has taken place and tickets are closed. No tickets exist and nothing can be bought.

Lantern Night is the hall's imaginary spring evening. The long tables are pushed to the walls, the shutters are closed and every lantern made over the winter is lit at once.

The evening begins with the smallest pieces on the lowest shelf and ends with the great paper boat that hangs above the door. Between the two there is time to walk slowly around the room, read the maker's cards and compare how thin rice paper and heavy cartridge paper carry the same lamp.

Warm drinks are described in the programme, although no cup has ever been filled. Soft shoes are suggested so that the floorboards stay quiet while the readings take place.
