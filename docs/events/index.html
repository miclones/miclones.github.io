  • Paper Boat Evening 2025, 20 June 2025

Evenings at the hall are small and slow. A few lanterns are lit, somebody reads the cards aloud, and the benches are moved into a half circle so that everyone can see the far wall where the larger pieces hang.

Each gathering has a theme, such as boats, orchards or weather, and guests are invited to notice how the same sheet of paper looks entirely different once a lamp is placed behind it. Notes about every evening are collected in a binder near the door, together with sketches of the layout and a short list of what went well.

Rainy evenings are moved indoors to the side room, where the ceiling is lower and the light feels warmer.
