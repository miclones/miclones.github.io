Opening hours

The hall keeps imaginary hours that follow the daylight. In spring the shutters open when the first light reaches the drying racks, and they close after the benches have been straightened.

Summer afternoons run longer, so the lanterns are lit later and the side room stays cool with both windows open. Autumn brings shorter sessions and an extra pot of paste, since damp air slows the glue.

Winter is for repairs. Torn panels are patched, loose threads are retied and the binder of notes is copied out afresh before the new year begins.
